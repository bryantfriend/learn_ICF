REFERENCE & LIVE PIPELINE SANDBOX

One action.
Six clear steps.

A button says “Borrow a book.” Your app needs to decide whether that should happen. ICF makes that decision easy to follow.

I · IntentWhat someone wants to do.

C · ContextThe verified facts we need.

F · FlowThe steps that carry it out.

ICF is the Intent-Centric Framework. Use an Intent for meaningful state changes, like a purchase or completing a lesson. A purely visual action, like opening a dropdown, can stay simple.

    04 / MAKE IT YOURS
    From this mission to your project
    
      
    1. Pick one meaningful action. Name it with a verb, such as BorrowBookIntent. Write down who acts, what they send, and what should change.
    2. Copy the template. Copy the contents of Template/ into your project. From the copied root, run node ICF/examples/checkPipeline.mjs to see the successful and failed traces.
    3. Declare the six stage groups. Use named helpers in every group. Even a no-op group needs an explicit step returning { ok: true, intent: intent }.
    4. Build one helper at a time. Add domain helpers under the relevant stage’s Domain folder and expose them through its registry. Keep the Intent as a readable stage map.
    5. Check both outcomes. Test a success, invalid input, denied permission, and a processing failure. Find the failed stage and step in the declaration. Inspect stored state before retrying a request that reached Process.
